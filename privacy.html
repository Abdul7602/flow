Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Flow ("the app", "we") is a personal notes, tasks, and calendar application. This policy explains what information Flow handles, where it goes, and what control you have over it. The short version: your data is yours, we collect the minimum needed to make the app work, and we don't sell or share it with advertisers.

What we collect

  • Email address — used solely to create your account and send you login codes.
  • Your content — the notes you write and the tasks, dates, times, and locations extracted from them.
  • Settings — your review time, timezone, and notification preferences.
  • Push subscription — a technical token that lets us deliver your daily review notification to your device (only if you enable notifications).

We do not collect your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, or any advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics trackers or advertising SDKs.

Where your data lives

Your account and content are stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) in the European Union (EU West) region. Access is protected by row-level security: only your authenticated account can read or modify your data.

AI processing

Flow's core feature is understanding what you write. To do this, the text of a note is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the extracted tasks, dates, and locations. This happens through our secure server function — your identity is verified first, and the AI provider receives only the note text needed for extraction, not your email or account details.

Per Anthropic's API terms, data sent to the API is not used to train their models.

Emails

Login emails (magic links and codes) are sent via Resend from the address login@flow-daily.com. We send no marketing emails.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, we send one daily review reminder at the time you choose. You can turn this off anytime with the toggle in the app's settings, which deletes your push subscription from our servers.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share your notes with third parties beyond the AI processing described above.
  • We never show ads or use your content for advertising.

Data deletion

You can delete individual notes and tasks in the app at any time — deletion is immediate and permanent, including from our servers. To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at privacy@flow-daily.com and we will remove everything within 30 days.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact privacy@flow-daily.com to exercise any of these rights.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will note it in the app. The latest version always lives at this address.
