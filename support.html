Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Open Flow → Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your notes, tasks, and account data.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID (iOS) or Google Play (Android) account settings, not inside Flow directly.
  • Is my data private? Yes — see our Privacy Policy for full details.
